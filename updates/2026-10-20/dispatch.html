What This Update Actually Means

Live

New Event Content: Little Devil, Arch Demon, Midnight Circus, Walnut Feast

Four new named rows appear in the data: 10x · Little Devil, 10x · Arch Demon, Midnight Circus and Walnut Feast. Two Record entries also show up: Record - Last Fort: Survival and Record - Last Train: Survival.

The data gives names only. Nothing here says what these do, how they work, or what rewards they carry. The "10x" prefix on the first two looks like a multiplier or pull-count label, but that is a guess from the naming alone. Midnight Circus fits the Halloween-season timing, but that is also an inference.

Live

Pumpkin Gift Bundle and Wasteland Store

Pumpkin Gift Bundle shows up in five changed rows, and Wasteland Store is also touched. Rows that change like this are usually event or shop configs being switched back on, not new features.

Five Pumpkin Gift Bundle rows probably means several price or tier variants of one bundle. The data doesn't show contents or prices.

Live

Crimson Crystal and Crimson Source Troop Tiers

Defender, Commando and Archer (listed as Rifleman Lv. 10) each get changed rows. Each has a Lv. 10 entry, four Crimson Crystal tiers, and one Crimson Source entry.

The pattern is identical across all three troop types, so this looks like one shared upgrade ladder applied to each. The data doesn't include stats or costs, so what the tiers actually change is unknown.

Live

Vehicle Customization Art

Seven images tied to changed data use the "gaizhuangche" naming, which is likely pinyin for a modification or customization vehicle. Two are list icons (07_2 and 08_2). Others are formation and exterior art named "fenseyinfu" and "nihong", plus a resource-shortage shop image.

These are linked to changed data, so they are wired in. The filenames suggest a vehicle skin or customization set with a shop tie-in, but the evidence doesn't confirm that. Treat the translation as a best guess.

Staged

Unlinked Art: Gift Banners, Frame, Buff Icons

Several image sets are present but not connected to any changed data. These include four gift-shop banners, a customization-vehicle background, collection button states, and two buff icons (Buff_A_Arista, Buff_F_FireDown). There is also an item frame pair, icon_item_Frame_dakehu2610, which looks like a cosmetic frame tagged 2610.

None of this is confirmed. Staged art can ship later, change, or never appear.
