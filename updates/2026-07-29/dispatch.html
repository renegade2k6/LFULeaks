What This Update Actually Means

Staged

Hanna & Sasha

40 new images this update, and the bulk of the identifiable ones center on two apparent new characters: Hanna and Sasha. Both have half-body and head portrait art, and Hanna additionally gets a full 5-icon set plus a red "exclusive show" variant icon.

None of this art is linked to any changed data table yet, so there's no kit, stats, or release mechanism attached. Sasha in particular only has portrait art so far, nothing else.

Staged

Silence Debuff Icon

A new debuff icon (Buff_D_Silence) showed up alongside the Hanna/Sasha art, unlinked to any data change. Could belong to one of the new heroes' kits, could be unrelated housekeeping art. No way to tell yet.

Partial

Summer Special, Fashion Icon, Mystery Box, Elite Medal, Lucky Purchase

The rest of this update's 24 changed tables are old event configs cycling back on: Summer Special (touched five times in this diff alone), Fashion Icon, Mystery Box Machine, Advanced Elite Hero Selection Medal, and Lucky Purchase.

These aren't new features. They're recurring banners/shops getting their config refreshed for another run.
