The breakdown

What This Update Actually Means

Live

Beach Festival

Beach Festival is confirmed as a new live event, with a full set of summer cosmetics, chest icons, anniversary talk bubbles, and formation-screen decorations wired to real data this update.

This is the clearest new content in the patch, backed by both a new row and a broad spread of linked assets.

Partial

Water Dragon / Ice Dragon

Two new monster entries, Ice Dragonling and Ice Dragon Prince, are linked to data along with a few dragon icons. But a much larger batch of Water Dragon backgrounds, buttons, grids, and title assets found this update have no data link at all.

Reads like the icy half of this dragon pair is being set up now, with a separate water-themed dragon still waiting in the wings.

Partial

Summer Pack / Battle Pass

A summer battle-pass banner and background are linked to data, but the bulk of the pass UI, cost buttons, pay button, shell icon, and title art, is unlinked staged images.

Expect the shell of a summer battle pass to go live soon, but the purchase flow isn't wired up yet.

Partial

Frame Rewards

Three item-frame icons (jingcaidaren, jingcaixinxiu, yuyanfashi) are linked to reward or item data. Matching UI frame variants of the same names showed up as unlinked images.

Likely the same three cosmetic frames, just with a second art pass staged for later.

Live

Recurring Event Configs

Several rows changed this update are repeats: Summer Special appears five times and Summer Chill, Wasteland Store also cycle back. A long list of skin entries (Blanche, Enrique, Quinn, and others) also changed, consistent with a rotating shop or banner refresh rather than new characters.

None of this points to new mechanics, it's the usual seasonal rotation machinery turning over.
