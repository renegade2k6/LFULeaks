What This Update Actually Means

Live

Anniversary 2026 Event

A new Activity_panelTable entry (#10160202) wires in a full Anniversary 2026 banner and background, plus a big-picture gift pack backdrop reused across five older activity rows. New Iris and Thomas equipment art (body, helmet, jewelry, weapon) rides along with it.

This reads as the main anniversary celebration event, not just a reskin — new panel, new banner, new equip sets tied to specific heroes.

Live

Hero 'Plus' Skins: Ada, Boton, Mira, Clara

Four new Hero_skin_listTable rows add 'Plus' variant skins for Ada, Boton, Mira, and Clara — full head icon, skin art, and recruit banner assets for each.

All four follow the same naming pattern and dropped in the same update, so expect them bundled as a themed skin line rather than a single spotlight release.

Live

Honor Level System Expansion

Maincity_levelTable picks up new rows (#131–154+) introducing honor level icons and star tiers — five star ranks layered on top of at least five new honor level brackets, each with its own icon and background.

This looks like an expansion to an existing prestige/level system rather than a brand-new feature, giving high-level players more granular tiers to climb.

Live

Weekend Card Battle Pass

A new Activity_panelTable row (#273206) adds a 'WeekendCardBp' banner and tab image. Naming and the linked 'bg_weekpass' art in the staged pile suggest a short-run weekend battle pass, separate from the anniversary track.

Staged

Anniversary Cosmetics & Frames

A batch of anniversary-themed cosmetic art (halos, wings, nameplates, chat bubble, avatar frame, ride reskin) sits in the files with no confirmed data hookup yet — same for a standalone reward-frame icon pair.

These are almost certainly meant for the Anniversary 2026 event above, but until they link to actual table data, treat them as prepared assets, not confirmed rewards.
